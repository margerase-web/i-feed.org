About IFEED

IFEED provides data on Korean government and National Assembly policy for SMEs and startups, for academic use.

Overview

The Institute for Entrepreneurial Ecosystem Development (IFEED) is an innovation policy think tank. It specialises in research on entrepreneurial ecosystem policy for SMEs and startups, and in academic cooperation.

We combine the rigour of academic theory with practical insight from the policy field to identify policy agendas suited to a fast-changing environment for SMEs and startups, and to design evidence-based policy alternatives.

Research scope

Our research centres on the Korean government’s policy for SMEs and startups. We compare it with the innovation and entrepreneurial ecosystem policies of the UK, the US, the European Union and other major economies, and develop policy solutions that work at national, regional and global level.

Core research themes

  1. 01

    Sustainable Entrepreneurial Ecosystems

    In line with global sustainability agendas such as climate tech, the circular economy and the ESG transition, we study how to grow sustainable startups that build long-term resilience and create social and economic value at the same time.

  2. 02

    Global and Transnational Ecosystems

    We analyse policy for open innovation ecosystems on the global stage: how born-global startups scale up, the inflow of cross-border venture capital, and the movement of talent and technology across borders.

  3. 03

    Digital and AI-Driven Ecosystems

    We examine the structural changes that AI, deep tech and the platform economy bring to startups, investment and incubation, and propose data-driven policy for the next generation of startups.

  4. 04

    Inclusive and Place-Based Ecosystems

    We look for entrepreneurial ecosystem policy that eases concentration in the capital region and, through smart specialisation linked to each region’s industries, lets people and places across the country grow together.

  5. 05

    Ecosystem Governance and Policy Diagnostics

    We test the direct and indirect effects of public policy and support schemes on the entrepreneurial ecosystem, from several angles, and propose evidence-based improvements.

Identity

IFEED

IFEED is short for the Institute for Entrepreneurial Ecosystem Development. The wordmark sets the short name beside the full name.
